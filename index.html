About

The person behind the projects.

I'm Mrithul — born in India, but I've spent most of my life elsewhere. Nine years in the UAE, five back in India, then three in Nairobi, Kenya, before returning to India for university. Moving between countries teaches you things no classroom can: how to read a room quickly, find common ground with people from completely different backgrounds, and get comfortable being the new person. I'm not naturally the loudest in a room — I take a little time to settle in — but once I do, I work well with people and tend to step up.

That played out pretty literally in Kenya. I started senior high, got involved in clubs and committees, and somewhere along the way became the unofficial tech guy — building tools with the team to make things more paperless and efficient around the school. By the second half of Year 12 I was deep in it, and by Year 13 I was chairing the Media Hub and Careers Committee. We kept pushing to do more with the school's systems, to the point where I'm fairly sure we drove the IT admin up the wall. But things got built, and people used them.

My interest in tech goes back to 8th grade — a school subject on AI was enough to make me think computers were genuinely cool. It stayed a hobby for years though, something fun on the side rather than a direction. I didn't see it as a career yet, which is how I ended up picking a subject combination at A-Levels that had nothing to do with it. Not a bad combo, just not the right one for where my head actually was.

Then Year 13 happened — the projects, the team, the building — and the spark came back properly. I knew I wanted to work in tech, specifically in AI and data science. BCA made sense as the path in without the maths and physics background I didn't have. The degree isn't the point; the direction is. Web development is a tool I'll always use, but building systems that learn, analyse, and solve real problems is what I'm here for.
